Illustrative Monod / Luedeking–Piret-type model: growth on glucose halts once nitrogen is depleted at the chosen onset time, after which excess carbon (from feed) is diverted to intracellular PHB granule synthesis, capped near 75% of dry cell weight. DO is a simplified function of agitation, aeration and oxygen demand — for engineering intuition, not process validation.
